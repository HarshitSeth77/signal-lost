CREW HANDBOOK / 01

Talk. Listen. Survive.

You are the crew of a failing spaceship. Survive all five sectors together.

  1. Create a room and share the code. Join with 2–6 separate devices.
  2. Keep a voice call open, or talk in person. Voice chat is not built into this version.
  3. Every player gets instructions for someone else's station. Read them aloud, including the station name.
  4. Listen for instructions for your own station, then press the matching control. A correct setting earns 100 points; a mistake costs 10 hull.
  5. Fix every system before the timer ends. Unresolved systems cost 15 hull each. The captain advances the crew between sectors.
  6. Complete the final sector with hull remaining to win. Faster clears earn bonus points.

Disconnected? Refresh to reconnect on this browser. Rooms last up to 3 hours of inactivity and reset when the local server restarts.
