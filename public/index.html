ECHO PROTOCOL / 03

Eight stages. One crew.

  1. Join the same room from 2–6 phones or computers and ready your pilot.
  2. Private screens matter: read clues aloud, guide the blind pilot and compare secret transmissions.
  3. New stages include Blind Navigator, Split Realities, Impostor Transmission and Alien Sound Hunt.
  4. Use the on-screen D-pad on mobile or arrow/WASD keys on a computer.
  5. Clear all eight stages, regroup inside the final ship and reach extraction together.

Headphones and crew voice are recommended for the sound hunt and social missions.
